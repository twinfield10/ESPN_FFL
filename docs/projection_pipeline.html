The architecture, in one sentence

Projections are blended as stat lines, then scored

Every source is reduced to a stat line — passing yards, receptions, sacks — never to points. Points are computed last, by running that blended stat line through each league's own scoring rules, read live from ESPN.

That ordering is the whole reason one pipeline serves nine leagues with different rules, including one that scores tackles for loss and another that pays a two-keeper $250 auction. A source that published points would have to be re-derived nine times, and could not be.

The weighting rule

One equal vote per source that has an opinion

Every source carries the same nominal weight. What varies is who turns up: a source with no real line for a player is flagged, drops out, and the survivors renormalise. Equal nominal weights make that renormalisation literal — the blend is always 1/n over whoever actually priced the player.

Measured on receiving yards across 1,285 draftable players. The 739 with no source are the deep bench nobody projects — they carry an ADP and nothing else.

Equality is the assertion, not the number. Set every source to 0.2 instead of 0.25 and nothing about the blend changes, because renormalisation only ever sees ratios. Set one of them to 0.3 and the rule is broken. The test guarding this asserts equality, not a total — an earlier version asserted the weights summed to 1.0 and broke twice in one day as sources joined.

TOMCAT, and why it is one source and not three

Touches · Opportunity · Market · Context · Availability · Tiers. The six words are the model's feature families rather than a decoration: touches is raw volume; opportunity is expected rather than realised production, which beats actual at predicting next week (R² 0.2907 against 0.2702); market is implied totals, implied points allowed and the spread; context is depth chart, scheme, play-caller and red-zone role — the depth chart being the only feature that has ever moved this model; availability is expected games, carried beside the projection rather than inside it; and tiers are the non-linear scoring ladders, integrated over a weekly distribution rather than evaluated at the season mean.

A kicker and a team defence are positions like any other — they start every week and they need a projection. That they are modelled differently underneath is an implementation detail: the usage arm reads snap share, the defence arm reads implied points allowed, the kicking arm reads red-zone volume. Above that line they are one opinion, cast once, weighted like everybody else's.

The kicking arm is the exception, and deliberately so. It ships as a readable column and is kept out of the blend until its field-goal channel clears the bar it was pre-registered against. One model, three arms, and the arms mature at different rates.

The column prefix underneath is still USG_. Renaming it would orphan Data/G2/2026/ — the pre-season counterfactual archived to answer gate G2 after the season is played, and the one artifact here that cannot be rebuilt. So the prefix rename needs a compatibility shim on that read rather than a find-and-replace, and it is not worth doing before a draft.

The consequence

Nominal weights are not realised weights

Because a flagged cell forfeits its share, the weight a source actually carries is computed per row and depends entirely on who else showed up. Averaged over draftable rows where the stat is live:

FantasyPros runs ahead of its nominal quarter because it reaches more players than anyone else. Before its fence lifted it was real for 60, and the same table read ESPN 0.55–0.65 — the blend was ESPN with three thin second opinions. It is a genuine multi-source blend now.

Not projections

What else reaches the board

Three layers feed the board without entering the blend. They describe players rather than predicting them, and none of them moves TRUE_Points.

  1. The market

    One kona_player_info request per league returns ADP, auction values and a 45-stat projection, carrying player.id so the join is exact. Replacement level comes from each league's real starting slots; tiers are 1-D KMeans within position.

  2. Injuries, as duration rather than status

    A six-rung severity ladder resolves body part, expected weeks out, recurrence risk and a form cost. Diagnostics only — the fitted recovery curve was measured and rejected by its own pre-committed gates, so the columns ship and nothing is multiplied.

  3. Owner tendencies

    5,748 picks across 36 league-seasons: positional lean by round, NFL-team preference, rookie appetite, autodraft rate, auction budget shape. Every measurement is leave-one-out against the room and season-matched.

Two reading rules

Us is TRUE_Points — every rank, tier and VOR on the table is built from it. DST renders only when defences are in view; a team defence's projection is not missing from a receiver's row, it is a question never asked of him.

Compare TOM by rank rather than points. USG_Points is injury-adjusted and TRUE_Points is not, so the rank delta is the comparison that survives.
