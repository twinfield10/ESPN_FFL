Replaces the notebook-plus-Google-Sheets workflow. Split into three because the
foundation blocks the other two and they have different dependencies.

Plan 07 is **done**. The store exists at `Data/Store/<season>/<league_key>/`,
`python -m Scripts.refresh` builds it, and `streamlit run app/main.py` reads it.
Both view plans are unblocked.

**Plan 09's draft board page landed 2026-08-07** — `app/pages/draft_board.py` over
`app/draft_view.py`: scarcity curve, tier runway, value-on-the-board, and the full
filterable table sorted by value rather than rank. Renders for all nine leagues.
Its two remaining pages are **Live Draft** (not started, the one page that polls
ESPN in the render path) and **Draft History** (blocked on roadmap Phase 1's
backfill). Building it found three defects in `board.parquet` itself, all fixed —
including **every per-source point column being NaN on every board** — see the
plan's postscript.

The measured gap that justified it: ~8s per league to rebuild pre-season, rising
toward 22.9s with a full season of box scores, against 11ms to read the frame
back from parquet. It also closed an existing gap — ESPN data was never persisted
anywhere.

Note on 07, now done: it could not be built at first. `clean_pinny`/`clean_bol`
read the season's weekly props unconditionally, and those do not exist
pre-season — so `clean_lineups` raised `FileNotFoundError` every August. They now
degrade to an absent source, which plan 03's renormalisation already handles
correctly. Four smaller things surfaced with it, including a `pd.concat` index
leaking into parquet as a 470th column, and a same-day regression in `304ba39`
that made **all eight `FA_*` Google Sheet tabs fail silently** — no published
Sheet was affected, but nothing would have reported it. See the plan's postscript.

Plan 07's last deferred item is now done too: `populateGoogleSheet.py` reads the
store rather than re-ingesting, so there is one ingest path and Sheets cannot
disagree with the app. What is left of the Sheets question is
[plan 14](14-thin-google-sheets.md) — and the answer turned out to be *keep it*,
because the Sheet is readable on a phone away from home and five of the eight
published leagues belong to other owners.

## Suggested order

**Before the draft:** ~~01~~ → ~~10~~ → ~~11~~ → ~~05 (espn-api only)~~ →
~~02~~ → ~~07~~ → ~~15~~ → ~~**09 board**~~ → **09 Live**. All the numbers the
draft board is built on are done, the board itself exists at
`Data/Store/<season>/<league>/board.parquet`, and **the page over it is built** —
so there is a usable draft-day artifact today. What is left before the draft is
[09's Live page](09-frontend-draft-views.md#2-live-draft), which is upside rather
than a blocker: the board on a second monitor works without it, which is the
plan's own build-order argument.

Note on 11, now done: it turned out to matter beyond GOP. The per-slot fix
also stopped offensive players in *every* league being credited for imputed
kick-return yards and points-allowed tiers at D/ST rates.

Note for 05, now answered: **0.46.0 does not fix** the shared-scoring-dict bug,
so `fetch_utils.isolate_scoring_format()` stays. Nor does it fix the per-slot
override handling that [plan 11](11-per-slot-scoring.md) works around.
`tests/test_espn_api_contract.py` asserts both are still upstream, so a future
release that fixes either will fail the suite and prompt removal.

**Before week 1:** 03 → 04 → 08 → 13. Plan 13 needs posted game lines, so it
cannot be finished in August — 51 of 272 games had lines on 2026-08-03. Its
distributional-scoring piece (`E[f(X)]` over tiers) can be built and tested
against 2025 now, independent of 2026 lines.

**Projection quality (the current focus):** ~~16 step 1 (crosswalk)~~ →
~~[16 Step 0](16-usage-data-layer.md#step-0--the-gates-measured-2026-08-06) (the
gates)~~ → ~~[16 step 3, availability](16-usage-data-layer.md#extraction-layer)~~ →
~~[16 step 4 + 18 (the season head)](18-season-usage-model.md)~~ →
[**19**](19-weekly-usage-model.md) (weekly, comes online ~week 3) →
[20](20-consensus-sources.md) → [03](03-projection-source-coverage.md).
G2 for the season head waits on the 2026 season being played.

**Step 3 is done, 2026-08-07.** `R/GetContext.R` + `Scripts/usage/context.py`
backfill rosters, injuries, snap counts and depth charts for 2016–2025 and pull 2026
as far as upstream allows. The scheduling fact it turned up matters for the draft:
**injuries, snap counts and depth charts cannot be pulled for 2026 at all yet** —
nflreadr refuses them while `most_recent_season()` is 2025 — while the 2026 roster
snapshot is served and updated daily. So the pre-season head's availability input is
trailing games played plus roster status, not a current designation.

And that input is weaker than [plan 18](18-season-usage-model.md) assumed. Prior-season
games played predicts next season at r = +0.663 over the whole pool but only
**+0.343 among players who managed 8+ games**, which is the population a board ranks
— the full-pool figure is mostly separating reserves from starters.

**Step 4 and the season head landed the same day.** `Scripts/usage/features.py`
builds the as-of season features, `Scripts/usage/season.py` fits volume × efficiency
× expected games and emits `USG_<stat>` lines, and `python -m Scripts.usage.backtest`
runs the 2019–2025 walk-forward. It beats the naive draft heuristic on ordering at
RB/WR/TE and on top-N hit rate at all four positions, with 12% better MAE on the
noisy rate stats — and does not improve yardage, and slightly hurts QB. It abstains
on 42% of rostered players, all 1,497 rookie rows among them. **It is not in
`WEIGHTS`**, because the comparison that would justify that — against the four-source
blend — cannot be run on history at all, and the 2026 board is the first chance.

**Step 0 is done, measured 2026-08-06.** It built a 5,257 player-week 2025
evaluation set out of all nine league stores, fitted the crudest usage baseline on
2016–2024, and published the pairwise residual-correlation matrix.
`python -m Scripts.usage.gates` regenerates all of it in 2.5s.

**G0 passed, G1 failed, and the failure is the useful part.** Usage residuals
correlate +0.832 with ESPN's where FantasyPros' correlate +0.988 — so the signal
is genuinely independent, and FantasyPros is the *least* independent source in the
blend, which is also plan 20's answer. But adding the crude model raises per-stat
MAE at every weight, so nothing is in `WEIGHTS`. On rows where the player actually
took snaps the effect is −0.16% to +0.35%, meaning essentially the whole deficit is
**not knowing who plays** — so availability features come first rather than fourth.

**The crosswalk is done.** `Rscript R/GetPlayerIDs.R` writes
`Data/NFL/player_ids.parquet` and `Scripts/crosswalk.py` reads it: 12,470 players,
7,908 `gsis_id`↔`espn_id` pairs. Measured **98.5-99.0% coverage of individual
players across all nine boards**, every miss a D/ST team unit. Draft boards now carry
`gsis_id`, so they are the bridge between ESPN's world and nflverse's.

It also resolves the collisions that motivated it: the two Lamar Jacksons (Ravens QB
`00-0034796`, cornerback `00-0036152`) and the two Justin Jeffersons now hold
distinct ids. Ambiguous upstream ids — 13 `espn_id`, 10 `gsis_id` where one id
covers two players — are refused rather than joined on, because a merge on a
duplicated key fans out rows and would silently shift every positional rank below
the duplicate.

[Plan 16](16-usage-data-layer.md) is the shared data and feature layer plus the
residual-independence gate; [18](18-season-usage-model.md) and
[19](19-weekly-usage-model.md) are the two model heads on top of it.
[Plan 17](17-draft-usage-model.md) is a superseded stub.

Plan 03's weight re-tune waits for 16, because re-deriving weights over four sources
and then again over five or more is the same work twice. The store makes the re-tune
tractable for the first time: `refresh --all --season 2025` yields one row per
player-week with the actual outcome, all four sources' stat lines, and 129
`*_is_imputed` flags — so weights can be fitted on cells where a source really had a
line rather than on its imputed copy of ESPN.

**Whenever:** 06, and the rest of 05. Plan 14 step 2.3 (`oauth2client` →
`google-auth`) is worth doing before the season rather than during it.
