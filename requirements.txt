# --- ESPN ---------------------------------------------------------------
# 0.46.0 is a hard floor, not a preference: scrape_player_stats reads
# player.__dict__['breakdown'] for raw per-week stats, and BoxPlayer only sets
# that attribute from 0.46.0. On 0.45.1 the raw stats sit under the misleading
# name 'points_breakdown', which 0.46.0 repurposed to hold applied points.
# tests/test_espn_api_contract.py enforces the floor and the semantics.
